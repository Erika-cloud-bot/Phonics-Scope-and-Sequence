# Grade 3 Ontario — Phonics, Word Study & Grammar Scope and Sequence

**How to use this template:** Each week pairs a word-study/phonics focus with a grammar focus from the Ontario 2023 Language curriculum (Strand B: Foundations of Language). Adjust pacing to your class — some patterns may need two weeks, others can be combined. Leave Weeks 1–2 and any post-break weeks lighter for routines and review.

---

## Term 1 (September–December) — Weeks 1–14

| Week | Word Study / Phonics Focus | Morphology Focus | Grammar Focus | Suggested Application |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Routines & diagnostic spelling check | — | Review: nouns, verbs, adjectives (Gr. 1–2 review) | Sentence scavenger hunt in a read-aloud |
| 2 | Closed syllables review; short vowel patterns | — | Common vs. proper nouns (review) | Label classroom nouns; capitalize proper nouns |
| 3 | Vowel teams: *ai, ay* | — | Collective nouns (*team, herd, family*) | Sort nouns: one thing vs. a group of things |
| 4 | Vowel teams: *ee, ea* | — | Abstract nouns (*joy, freedom, courage*) | Sort nouns: things you can touch vs. ideas/feelings |
| 5 | Vowel teams: *oa, ow* (long o) | Prefix *un-* | Personal pronouns: subject (*I, you, he, she, it, we, they*) | Pronoun substitution in sentences |
| 6 | Vowel teams: *oo* (two sounds) | Prefix *re-* | Personal pronouns: object (*me, him, her, us, them*) | Fix pronoun errors in sample sentences |
| 7 | R-controlled vowels: *ar, or* | Suffix *-ful* | Forms of "to be": *am, is, are* | Sentence frames with subject-verb agreement |

| 10 | Diphthongs: *ou, ow* (loud sound) | Suffix *-ness* | Superlative adjectives (*-est*) | "Most/-est" scavenger hunt (tallest, fastest…) |

| 12 | Review & consolidation | Prefix/suffix review | Capitalization: proper nouns, titles, days/months | Proofread a paragraph for capitalization |
| 13 | Hard/soft *c* and *g* | Prefix *dis-* | End punctuation: statements vs. questions vs. exclamations | Write one of each sentence type about a topic |
| 14 | Term 1 review & assessment | Term 1 review | Term 1 review: parts of speech + punctuation | Independent writing sample using all Term 1 skills |

### Notes for planning
- **Daily structure suggestion:** 10–15 min word study/phonics + 5–10 min grammar mini-lesson, both reinforced during the day's writing block.
- **Differentiation:** Use the same weekly pattern/focus across ability levels, but vary word complexity (e.g., simpler vowel-team words vs. multisyllabic versions) and sentence complexity in grammar tasks.
- **Assessment touchpoints:** Weeks 12, 14, 25–26, and 34–36 are natural points for quick checks or report card evidence.
- **Curriculum alignment:** This sequence supports Ontario Language 2023 expectations B2 (Language Foundations for Reading and Writing) and B3 (Language Conventions for Reading and Writing).
